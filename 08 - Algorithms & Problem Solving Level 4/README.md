# 08 - Algorithms & Problem Solving Level 4

## Course Overview

**Algorithms & Problem Solving Level 4** is a practical C++ course focused on solving advanced programming problems related to **Date & Time Manipulation**.

The course focuses on building date and time operations from the ground up. It covers leap years, days, weeks, months, years, date comparisons, date calculations, calendars, periods, business days, and date formatting.

The main goal is to develop the ability to solve date and time problems using logical thinking and reusable functions, then combine these functions in practical projects.

## What This Course Covers

### Introduction

The course starts with an introduction to the topics covered in the course and provides a Telegram community link for programming discussions.

### Years, Days, and Time Calculations

The early problems focus on basic calculations related to years, months, and days.

Topics include:

* Converting years into days, hours, minutes, and seconds.
* Checking whether a year is a leap year.
* Calculating the number of days in a month.
* Calculating the number of days in a year.
* Calculating hours, minutes, and seconds.
* Finding the day of the week for a specific date.
* Printing a monthly calendar.
* Printing a complete yearly calendar.

### Date Operations

The course then moves to working directly with dates.

Topics include:

* Finding the day order within a year.
* Converting a day order back into a date.
* Adding a number of days to a date.
* Comparing two dates.
* Checking whether one date is before another date.
* Checking whether two dates are equal.
* Checking whether one date is after another date.
* Validating a date.

### Advanced Date Calculations

The course contains a large set of problems for performing more advanced calculations and modifications on dates.

Topics include:

* Finding the difference between two dates.
* Calculating age using days, weeks, months, and years.
* Increasing dates by days, weeks, months, and years.
* Decreasing dates by days, weeks, months, and years.
* Working with different date ranges.
* Calculating business days.
* Calculating vacation days.
* Finding the return date after a vacation.

### Periods and Date Comparisons

The course also covers working with periods of time.

Topics include:

* Comparing time periods.
* Checking whether two periods overlap.
* Calculating the number of overlapping days.
* Checking whether a date is inside a specific period.
* Working with start and end dates.

### String and Date Conversion

The course covers converting dates between strings and date structures.

Topics include:

* Converting a string into a date.
* Converting a date into a string.
* Splitting date strings into their individual parts.
* Formatting dates for display.
